# Boss URL Shortener — Organic Content Strategy & Editorial Roadmap
**Project:** Boss URL Shortener SaaS  
**Goal:** Establish Topical Authority in URL Shortening, Link Analytics, and Digital Marketing  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## 1. Strategy Overview & Pillar Model

To rank against established URL shorteners in Google Search, Boss URL Shortener employs a **Hub-and-Spoke Content Architecture**:
- **Core Hub:** Main Landing Pages (`/`, `/free-url-shortener`, `/custom-url-shortener`, `/url-shortener-with-analytics`) targeting high-intent transactional queries.
- **Topical Spokes:** The Educational Knowledge Hub (`/blog`) containing in-depth, original guides answering informational queries. Every guide links contextually back to relevant product features.

## 3. Sustainable Editorial Workflow

- **E-E-A-T Assurance:** Articles are attributed to real engineering team members with defined roles.
- **Structured Data:** Every article embeds valid `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema.
- **Zero AI Slop Policy:** Content avoids generic repetitive filler, fake metrics, or keyword stuffing.
